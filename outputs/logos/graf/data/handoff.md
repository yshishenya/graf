# Graf Logo Handoff

Selected direction: larger, thin circular `Ф` symbol with purple equalizer.

Use the circular `Ф`-like mark as the logo symbol, app icon, and favicon. The three purple bars carry the audio/equalizer meaning without making the identity a literal microphone icon. This v3 version makes the `Ф` visibly larger, but keeps the ring, tabs, and bars slimmer so the mark stays elegant.

Preserve:

- large, thin circular `Ф` container;
- three purple equalizer bars inside the mark;
- flat vector-friendly construction;
- separate light and dark versions.

Avoid:

- standalone microphone icons;
- full-purple logo treatment;
- gradients, shadows, 3D, or folklore motifs;
- using the older generated raster crops as production source.

Files:

- final symbol light: `final-symbol/svg/graf-symbol-light.svg`
- final symbol dark: `final-symbol/svg/graf-symbol-dark.svg`
- app icon light: `final-symbol/app-icon/graf-app-icon-light.svg`
- app icon dark: `final-symbol/app-icon/graf-app-icon-dark.svg`
- favicon source: `favicon/favicon.svg`
- light favicon source: `favicon/favicon-light.svg`
- final manifest: `final-symbol/data/manifest.json`

Previous draft exports were removed after the v3 symbol became the source of truth.
