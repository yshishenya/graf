# Quickstart: Поделились со мной

1. Create a meeting in workspace A and grant a user from workspace B recipient
   access.
2. Sign in as that recipient and open `/shared-with-me` or
   `/desktop/shared-with-me`.
3. Verify the meeting appears once, with no source workspace or owner details.
4. Open the card and verify it uses the existing restricted shared-meeting page.
5. Revoke or expire the grant; refresh the list and verify the card disappears.
6. Verify a pending email invitation does not appear until accepted by the
   matching verified account.
