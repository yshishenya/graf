# Owner Value Loop

## Primary Loop

1. Owner signs in or remains in signed-out local policy state.
2. Desktop shell shows ready state, minimal connection/session/policy badge,
   and embedded server-owned account/workspace/recent meeting summaries.
3. Owner records with visible local indicator and one-action Stop.
4. After Stop, local package is saved and queue truth appears.
5. Upload status appears consistently in desktop and web.
6. Owner may alternatively upload an owned audio or common video/meeting file.
7. Server shows audio extraction and transcription progress.
8. Meeting review becomes available with transcript and playback context.
9. Notes become available with summary, decisions, and action items.
10. Degraded/failure/access/deletion states remain truthful.
11. Browser-only routes are handed off clearly.
12. Source/track provenance explains desktop separate tracks vs uploaded mixed/extracted audio.

## Copy Boundaries

- `Uploaded` means server accepted artifacts, not transcript ready.
- `Transcript ready` means transcript is available, not notes ready.
- `Deleted` means deleted everywhere 2brain Rec controls, not universal external erasure.
- Uploaded mixed media must not imply separate microphone/system tracks.
